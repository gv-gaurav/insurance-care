Find Your Cover Type

What Kind of Cover Are You Looking For?

Commercial vehicle insurance isn't one product — it depends heavily on how the vehicle is used. Start with the category that matches your situation.

  • Van Insurance*

For tradespeople, sole traders, and businesses running one or more vans for work.

  • Courier & Delivery Insurance*

For self-employed and contracted drivers carrying goods for payment — including app-based delivery work.

  • Taxi & Private Hire Insurance*

For licensed private hire and public hire drivers, including app-based platforms.

  • Fleet & Mini-Fleet Insurance*

For businesses running multiple vehicles under a single policy — from 3 vehicles to full corporate fleets.

  • HGV & Haulage Insurance*

For owner-drivers and businesses operating heavy goods vehicles, whether hauling for clients or carrying your own goods.

Why InsuranceCare

Built for People Who Want to Understand Their Cover, Not Just Buy It

Commercial vehicle insurance has a lot of easy-to-miss detail — the difference between "business use" and "hire and reward," or between haulage and own-goods cover, catches out plenty of otherwise careful business owners. We built this site to close that gap: clear, UK-focused guides written specifically for van owners, couriers, taxi drivers, fleet operators, and hauliers — not generic advice borrowed from car insurance content.

We're not a broker and we don't sell policies ourselves. When you ask to be contacted, your enquiry goes to a firm that's authorised to give you a real quote and, if you choose, arrange your cover. You can read more about how we're paid — we're upfront about it.
