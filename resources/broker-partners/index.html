Our Broker Partners Panel

To keep our guides independent and comply with financial regulations, we only introduce visitors to FCA-authorised insurance brokers and insurers on our panel. We do not arrange policies or provide regulated advice ourselves.

Why Our Panel Model Works

  • FCA-Authorised Only: Every broker or insurer on our panel is fully authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) to trade in the UK.
  • Specialist Vetting: We route enquiries to partners who focus specifically on your category (e.g. private hire taxi, mini-fleet, or HGV logistics), ensuring you speak to someone with specialized market access.
  • Strict Data Safety: Panel members are bound by data processing agreements under UK GDPR and cannot sell or pass your contact details to third parties outside of quoting.

Current Panel Setup

Our panel consists of several leading independent UK commercial vehicle insurance brokerages and specialist logistics underwriters. When you submit a request, your vehicle class, location, and callback requirements are scored to match you with a provider that fits your requirements.
