Insurance Comparison Guides

To help you understand the details of commercial vehicle insurance, we have prepared side-by-side comparison tables explaining standard definitions, vehicle scopes, and use categories.

Available Comparisons

  • Business Use vs Hire & Reward Cover — Learn when standard business use cover is insufficient and how carrying third-party goods requires specific hire and reward additions.
  • Private Hire vs Public Hire Taxi Insurance — Understand local taxi licensing rules and how booking methods (pre-booked vs rank hailing) affect premiums.
  • Fleet Insurance vs Individual Policies — Consolidate your business vehicles (cars, vans, HGVs) into a unified fleet structure starting at 3+ units.
  • Haulage vs Carriage of Own Goods Cover — Find out how own goods tools cover differs from professional third-party transport.
  • Mini-Fleet vs Standard Fleet Insurance — Analyze how smaller fleet schemes (3 to 10 vehicles) compare against corporate fleet setups.
