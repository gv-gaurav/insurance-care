Editorial Policy

Editorial Policy

This page explains how our content gets made, what standards it's held to, and what to do if you spot something that needs fixing.

What We Cover

Our content focuses specifically on commercial vehicle insurance in the UK — van, courier, taxi, private hire, fleet, HGV, and haulage cover, along with the practical questions that come with each (cost, exclusions, claims, renewals). We stay within this focus deliberately, rather than trying to cover general insurance topics we're not positioned to research properly.

How We Write

Every guide is written to explain a topic clearly, not to sell a specific outcome. That means:

  • We describe types of cover and how they generally work, rather than naming or ranking specific insurers or brokers as "best"
  • We don't build comparison tools that filter or score providers based on your answers — our articles inform, they don't quote or advise
  • Where we reference figures (such as typical cost factors), we describe general patterns rather than presenting specific numbers as guaranteed prices
  • We link to related guides on the site so you can build a fuller picture of a topic, rather than repeating the same explanation across multiple pages

Research and Accuracy

Our content is researched using public regulatory guidance (including FCA and ICO material), industry information, and general market knowledge. Because insurance products, regulations, and market conditions change, we review and update our content periodically — but if you're relying on specific figures or rules for a decision, we'd always recommend confirming the current position directly with a broker or insurer, since they'll have access to live, product-specific information we don't.

What Our Content Is Not

Nothing on this site is regulated financial or insurance advice. Our guides are general and educational — they're designed to help you understand a topic well enough to have a more informed conversation with a broker or insurer, not to tell you what to buy. Regulated advice and recommendations are only provided by the FCA-authorised firms in our broker and insurer panel.

Author Approach

Our content is produced by writers with UK insurance-market research experience, and reviewed for factual accuracy and compliance with our legal-lane boundaries before publishing.

Corrections

If you spot something inaccurate, outdated, or unclear in any of our guides, we want to know. Please contact us with the page URL and a description of the issue, and we'll review and correct it as needed.
